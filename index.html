GRAM PRACTICE 1 Two Truths and a Lie — Family Edition
Book Page 9 7 / 11

Write 6 sentences using Verb To Be: 3 about yourself and 3 about a family member. One sentence in each block must be a lie! The class will guess which one.

📝 Teacher's Example
1
I am a teacher.
2
I am not a rockstar.
3
I am a pilot. LIE!
About Yourself
I am a ___________.
I am not a ___________.
I am / am not ___________.
About a Family Member
My [brother/sister] is a ___________.
My [mom/dad] is not a ___________.
They are / are not ___________.
GRAMMAR 3 Verb TO BE — Yes/No Questions
Book Page 10 8 / 11
The Verb JUMPS to the beginning!
You are a student. → Are you a student?
Pronoun
Question
Short Answer

Am I late?
Yes, you are. / No, you're not.

Are you a student?
Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

Yes, he is. / No, he's not.

Is she from Paris?
Yes, she is. / No, she's not.

Yes, it is. / No, it's not.

Are they friends?

⬆️ Rising intonation at the end of every question! "Are you a student ↗?"
GRAM PRACTICE 2 Guess Who?
Book Page 10 9 / 11
One student secretly chooses a character. Another student has a maximum of 4 Yes/No questions to guess who it is. Only short answers allowed: "Yes, I am / No, I'm not / Yes, she is / No, he isn't."
💬 Sample Questions
Are you 25 years old?
Is she a teacher?
Are you from Mexico?
Is he a doctor?
Are they from Canada?
EXIT TICKET The Fictional Alter Ego
Book Pages 8–10 10 / 11
Choose a famous character (cartoon, movie, TV show) and say 3 sentences about yourself as that character. Use at least one affirmative and one negative sentence. Include A / AN!
📝 Your Template
I am [character name].
I am a / an [occupation/description].
I am not [something false about the character].
🎬 Teacher's Example
Homer Simpson
I am Homer Simpson.
I am a father and a worker.
I am not smart — but I am funny!
